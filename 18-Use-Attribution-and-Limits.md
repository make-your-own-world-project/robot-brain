# Public-use, credit, and privacy notes

![Public information can still affect privacy when separate clues are joined together.](illustrations/public-context-sovereignty-boundary.png)

This repository contains the public explanation of the project, the problems that led to it, the working design, recorded results, and remaining work. It does not contain the private records used by the installation or the details needed to reach running private services.

## How claims are made

Statements about current abilities are based on named project records and tests. A claim covers the part and conditions described by that evidence. It is not expanded into a promise about every record, person, installation, or future version.

Descriptions of commercial language-model failures report what an assistant did and what happened next in dated project sessions. They do not assign a motive to a provider or claim knowledge of an undisclosed internal cause.

The documents describe language-model training as destructive when useful patterns remain but the complete human meaning and history cannot be recovered from the trained model. This does not mean that the original work was erased everywhere or that a provider kept no separate copy.

That description is not a legal accusation of theft and does not decide whether any particular material was copied, licensed, permitted, or used lawfully. Those questions depend on the material, the use, the applicable terms, and the law.

## Privacy and other people

The public files leave out private records and details that could identify someone alone or when combined with other clues. They also leave out employer and customer information, passwords, private connection details, and instructions for reaching private records or running services.

Control of one's own records does not grant control over another person. Permission to use personal material does not create permission to publish another person's identity, words, private facts, image, or a language model's guesses about them.

Outside text, images, software, research collections, public standards, names, and marks keep their own rights and terms. Credit is not a substitute for permission when permission is required. The license for this project's documents applies only to the material it identifies.

## Help from language models

Language models helped with research, drafting, coding, and review under human direction. The publisher chose the sources, checked the claims, accepted or rejected the wording, and approved the files released here.

No language-model provider sponsors or endorses the project. Model responses used during development were not used to train a replacement general language model.

These documents explain a personal software project and the evidence recorded during its development. They are not legal, medical, financial, employment, security, or other professional advice.

These documents describe recorded capabilities and the evidence behind them. They do not give professional advice or guarantee a result in another person's situation. Anyone reusing the software must evaluate the privacy, security, rights, and operating rules that apply there.
